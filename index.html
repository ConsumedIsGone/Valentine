My Dearest Riddhu

A Letter From My Heart

So here's the thing: Valentine's Day is supposedly about love and romance and all that stuff, but honestly? Every single day with you feels like Valentine's Day already. (Okay that was cheesy even for me, but it's TRUE so deal with it.)

But since the calendar says it's officially the day to be all mushy and romantic, I figured I should ask you properly: Will you be my Valentine?

I know, I know, we've been together for almost 3 years and you're literally my wifey atp, so this question seems kinda redundant. But hear me out!!! I want to ask you every single year, every single Valentine's Day, because choosing you isn't just a one time thing. It's something I want to keep doing over and over again.

You make my life feel like someone turned up the brightness and saturation settings to maximum. Everything is just more when I'm with you. More colorful, more exciting, more meaningful. Even the boring stuff like eating lunch becomes an adventure when you're doing that thing where you're mimicking me and randomly dancing (which is NOT as funny as you think it is... okay fine, it's hilarious).

I love how you make me laugh until my stomach hurts. I love how you get excited about the smallest things. I love your terrible jokes that I pretend not to laugh at (but secretly do). I love how you look at me like I'm the best thing since maggi, even when I'm being a complete disaster of a human being.

You deserve someone who will love you through every season: through your winter moods and your spring energy and your summer adventures. And I want to be that person. I want to be the one who holds your hand through everything, who celebrates your wins, who makes you laugh when you're sad, and who reminds you every single day that you're absolutely incredible.

So what do you say? Will you be my Valentine? Not just today, but for all the days that come after? Because I can't imagine doing this whole life thing with anyone else but you.

I love you more than I can fit into words (and trust me, I tried girl this is like my fifth draft 😭😭). You're my favorite person, my best friend, and the reason my heart does that stupid happy flutter thing.

Forever Yours,
Tanmay
